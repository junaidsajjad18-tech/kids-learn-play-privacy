Kids Learn & Play – Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 7, 2026

Welcome to Kids Learn & Play. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the privacy of children and families who use our application.

Important: Kids Learn & Play does not contain advertisements and does not use AdMob or other advertising services.

1. Information We Collect

Kids Learn & Play is designed as a children's educational application. We do not require users to create an account or provide personal information such as their name, email address, phone number, address, photographs, or other personal identifying information.

The application does not intentionally collect personal information from children.

2. Children's Privacy

Kids Learn & Play is designed for children and families. We take children's privacy seriously.

The application does not knowingly collect personal information from children. We do not knowingly request children to provide personal information.

3. Information Stored on the Device

Certain application settings and learning progress may be stored locally on the user's device. This may include information such as completed lessons, scores, stars, preferences, and application settings.

This information is used only to provide the application's learning and gameplay features and is not intended to personally identify the user.

4. Internet and Online Services

The core educational features of Kids Learn & Play are designed to function without requiring users to create an online account.

We do not sell, rent, or trade users' personal information.

5. Advertising

Kids Learn & Play does not display advertisements.

The application does not use Google AdMob or other third-party advertising networks.

6. Analytics and Tracking

Kids Learn & Play does not intentionally use third-party advertising trackers or tracking technologies to identify individual children.

7. Third-Party Services

The application does not require third-party advertising services to provide its core educational functionality.

If third-party services are added in a future version, this Privacy Policy will be updated to accurately describe the relevant data practices before or when such services are introduced.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information associated with the application. Since the application does not require users to create accounts or submit personal information, there is no user account database maintained by the application for such information.

9. Parents and Guardians

Parents and guardians who have questions about the application's privacy practices are welcome to contact us using the contact information provided below.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time if the application's features or data practices change.

Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.
